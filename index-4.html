Why travel with us

  • Your own crew

    A private guide and a private cook, plus porters who carry your luggage.

  • Fees are covered

    Park, conservation, rescue and camping or hut fees are already in the price.

  • Meals and camp comforts

    Meals all day on the mountain, with chairs and tables for breakfast, lunch and dinner.

  • A briefing before you climb

    We collect you at Kilimanjaro International Airport and take you to your hotel in Moshi. Your guide briefs you and checks your gear. Missing items can be rented that day.

  • Routes with a plan

    Where a route allows it, days are built around acclimatisation, such as climbing high and sleeping low at Lava Tower.

Mount Meru

At 4,562 m, Meru is Tanzania's second-highest mountain and a good climb before you take on Kilimanjaro. Four days, sleeping in huts.

  • Arusha National Park, with the most varied scenery of any Tanzanian mountain.
  • An armed ranger walks with your group because of the wildlife on the lower slopes.
  • Buffalo, giraffe, warthog, bushbuck and colobus monkeys along the trail.

        About us

        A Tanzanian trekking company based in Moshi, guiding climbers up Kilimanjaro and Mount Meru with a focus on safety, fair pay for our crew, and a genuinely good climb.

        • Local, licensed guides

          Every trek is led by a guide registered with the national park, who knows the mountain, the weather and the early signs of altitude sickness.

        • Fair treatment for our crew

          Porters carry within recommended weight limits and are properly fed, clothed and paid for every trek.

        • Small, personal groups

          You climb with your own private guide, cook and porters, not folded into a large group of strangers.

        • Safety first

          Guides carry a pulse oximeter and emergency oxygen, and check your health twice a day on the mountain.

        Why we do this

        Kilimanjaro changes people. We want every climber who trusts us with that experience to reach the summit safely, and every guide, cook and porter on our team to be treated and paid fairly for making it happen.

        Marangu route, summit day

        The Big Day begins at midnight

        You leave Kibo Hut around midnight for a long, strenuous climb, passing Hans Meyer Cave at 5,220 m. At sunrise you reach Gillman's Point on the crater rim (5,681 m), where the sun starts to warm the land. One to two more hours take you to Uhuru Peak at 5,895 m.
